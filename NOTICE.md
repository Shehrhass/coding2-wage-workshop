# Data and attribution

The workshop code and teaching material were adapted from [Coding 2: Python for
Data Analysis](https://github.com/ulrichwohak/Coding-2-Python-for-Data-Analysis).
The repository’s MIT license applies to the workshop materials.

The data are derived from the [US Census Bureau’s March 2005 Current Population
Survey Annual Social and Economic Supplement](https://www.census.gov/data/datasets/2005/demo/cps/cps-asec-2005.html).
